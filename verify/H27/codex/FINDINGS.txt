## Surprises

1. The T3 prompt matches the mock's summary pattern, so T3's reply is `H27-SUMMARY` (rollout line 57).
2. `compacted` carries a full `replacement_history`, and the initial context sits between the user messages rather than at the top.
3. `thread_history_1.sqlite` is a new store and the rollout header says `history_mode:"paginated"`. The store is only a projection of the rollout, but `threads.rollout_path` in `state_5.sqlite` decides where the rollout is looked up.
4. A hard kill leaves no trace of the interruption. A graceful one stores the `<turn_aborted>` message and sends it to the model on resume.
5. With defaults, Codex contacts chatgpt.com and GitHub on every start, even with a custom provider and no login.
